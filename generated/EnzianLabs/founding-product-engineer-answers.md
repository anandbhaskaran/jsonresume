# EnzianLabs (PEX) - Founding Product Engineer - Application Answers

## Why do you think you are the perfect fit for this role?

I have sat on both sides of the product PEX is building. For 7+ years I have invested in private markets (SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI, Polymarket, Blue Origin, among others), and I am convinced the space needs a tool that makes it simpler to understand and act on.

I have also been the engineer PEX needs right now. At Dronistics I was the founding engineer: I built the UI, backend, infra and CI/CD from scratch and kept it running through 500+ real deliveries. At LumApps I took multi-agent workflows from discovery to production alone, with eval pipelines, tracing, structured outputs and cost-per-request instrumentation, and I am on call for them. On the side I ship and run SwissNRI and Proplab solo, both document-heavy AI products built with AI coding tools I review line by line.

The vision I share with you: I started as a value investor and I still enjoy fundamentals. Today it is close to impossible to value a private company well. The data is fragmented across PDFs and inconsistent reports, and the methods themselves differ from public-market valuation: illiquidity, preference stacks, scenario-heavy outcomes. At the same time, the number of private-market participants is set to grow sharply as secondaries and retail access open up. Those three facts point to one product: something that combines document processing, AI and financial models so that complex information becomes something an investor can work with and trust. I believe the market needs it, and I would like to be the one building it.
